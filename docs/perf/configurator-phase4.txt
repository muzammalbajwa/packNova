Design your box (/configurator/), measured 2026-11-16 on a quiet machine (load average 2.8 to 3.5 before each run).
Chrome headless, node scripts/configurator-test.mjs --perf 1 [--mode mobile --cpu 4]

What changed: the old box stays on screen until the new one is ready; its textures are uploaded one per frame; its shaders are warmed
in a frame of their own (compile into a side scene); the swap frame is 0 to 3 ms; the old box is freed in a later frame. Building a box is
cheaper: paper grain is one cached tile instead of thousands of specks per texture, and the unprinted-sheet texture (never shown in the
configurator) is a 4 px swatch. Not changed: the first time a product type is chosen its code chunk loads and parses (one long task).
